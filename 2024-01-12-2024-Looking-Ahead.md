# 2024 – Looking Ahead

When I was writing the end of year company review it was soon apparent that there was an awful lot to talk about. 2023 had been an electric year for AI conversations in all its forms and 2024 was likely to be more so. To make things a little easier to follow I have written about each separately and this piece is solely concerned with what we may see in the forthcoming year.

## Old Meets New 

There are going to continue to be alignment issues in many respects when it comes to integrating new technologies into general society. As new processes and techniques are developed in conjunction with AI we will see the old replaced very quickly by those with the will and able to bear the costs of change. This is likely a reason why we see commerce as a whole preparing for a potentially tumultuous future. It would be prudent for governments to behave in the same way but they often tend to be slower to react to changes in social structures. 

Any period of transition is likely accompanied by certain stresses. Stresses caused by the old having to leave behind the old ways in order to survive the new reality. This is a lesson well understood by those who find a little sense of history useful. Larger and older institutions are normally well placed to know this and will take action to limit negative impacts of such changes while also looking to maximise any gains. When looking at the largest global institutions from different sectors you get a sense of what the hive mind is thinking. Actions belie words of intent. What people tell you they will do means nothing in light of what they actually do. Looking at the actions of the larger players you see caution, stabilisation and preparation.

They are likely very right to do so. Especially as there is a very real risk that the next big companies are being formed as we speak and that they may exist in very humble beginnings. Although this is somewhat fantastical still at this time, consider the task of needing a text editor, web browser or search engine. Even the text editor would have proven to be tricky to code just a few years ago for most. But now? Well now has changed a fair bit and you can generate a usable text editor by Large Language Model (LLM) typed tools already. Are they amazing and cover all use cases and are fully featured? Quite simply, no. They can be written from a simple prompt though and they do work in most instances. Sometimes good enough is exactly that. Ever wonder how much functionality actually gets used in an application such as Word? The point here is that soon there could easily be alternatives for software and hardware that were not considered possible before. Ones that do not rely on a monthly subscription fee and to be permanently online and report usage data. Ones that can be self-hosted and adapted as required.

## Testing for Truth 

Our societies are going to be exposed to an ever increasing amount of AI generated content. For many in a modern society there are a number of pressures that exist. Things that just have to be done. Despite all of our technological advancements there is still much in our lives that seems to take time away for many of us. Commuting, meetings, unscheduled work, complaints, problems and a host of others all take a bit of our time away. But many humans also feel compelled to be informed about the World. This has given us the modern forms of media with news and news-entertainment being a couple of subdivisions. 

The "truth" and "perceived truth" are going to be very tested. What is real anymore when it comes to any media distributed at some point over electronic means? When you can make anyone do and say anything you desire then what becomes real at that point? Perhaps more importantly how do you verify and check anything at all? Did that person really say that thing they were reported to have said? Or were they really at an event with a certain person? With the conventional types of media being mass produced from text prompts, just what are you going to be able to trust? This is only one aspect of this subject. There are a host of others and likely many that are far more important. This is what I am excited about the most in this coming year. What new paradigms are going to become prevalent in determining a truth in a deluge of data? This is something I covered briefly in my article from March 2023 that you can find here: Determining Truth in a Deluge of Data.

Although I name specifically media here it is something that will also apply to just about every other aspect of our lives. To our governments, legal systems, to the very way we do business on a daily basis. Is that call from a very heated and irate CEO demanding you transfer millions in funds actually them? Or is it a very targeted social engineering attack using the latest voice emulation software? The scope for harm or just mischief is simply vast.

New skills and methods are going to be urgently needed in order to address the changes to how we gather and process information about our World. This is something that will take time and I do not think it is something that we will get right first time for all cases. In fact I do not think it unreasonable to say that there are going to be times where things get very wrong.

Much of what we have been taught will need to be altered or at the very least better qualified. There is a tension between the goals of individuals, business and societies in goal alignment. Historically the issue has been resolved in many respects by the rules that are imposed by a society as to how individuals operate within it. During an education there is a huge amount of social rules and expectations that are conveyed by osmosis with little question. AI tooling will change that landscape greatly. There will be tools developed that are exclusively concerned with a users direct benefit. They will offer perfect memory about experiences, events, people and places in a way that extends the capacity of people. This information will have a value and it will be exchanged with others of an associated social group, or other interested entities.

In this we may well see a very interesting and possibly healthy change for society. There are many pundits in every industry or system of belief that have relied heavily on spouting a stream of alleged facts knowing that the speed and effort of verifying such information far exceeds that of the falsehoods delivered. AI partners in the future may go some way to quickly correct and prevent misinformation from travelling at quite the rate as has been historically seen.

## Legal Landscape 

Generally speaking the law is not very good at making predictive legislation. Trying to determine how new things will be used and abused is never an easy thing to do. 

Self-driving cars have yet to still be fully realised. There are a couple of things that have puzzled me greatly over the years in regards to driving. One is to do with the how the technology is being developed. There are many cars out there that are all very much "in development" and looking to crack the self-driving problem. Now this is fine for the developers of such technology and individual users are also required to accept terms and conditions, but what of everyone else? As a pedestrian have I accepted that I am now part of an experiment in order to help a privately owned company develop new technology? Well personally I am not entirely sure that I have. But perhaps even more interestingly is the very notion of personal transport in its own right. I remember The Jetsons promising me self-driving flying cars. Is that not a better solution than continuing with the 4 wheels paradigm? Would self-balancing 2 wheeled vehicles be better?

In the above case I simply hope to show that the creation of legislation when it comes to well-funded commercial ventures is a complex process that takes time, and in some cases lobbyists. This means that we will not see equality for all the changes that are likely to come down the pipeline. There is also the very real risk that legislation may be bought into play that is skipped entirely or even worse, hinders societal development.

It is very likely we will continue to see battles in respect to ownership in all its guises. This will especially be evident in markets such as entertainment. How technologies are constructed will become more of an issue. Chains of ownership or proof of fair use will be needed to bolster the integrity of Large Language Models (LLMs) that still have the World enamoured. There have been a number of instances that have already questioned the legal use of copyrighted material in the training of LLMs that are substantive enough to bring into question anything produced by the LLM. This makes things tricky to say the least for LLM producers. Just how do you prove that you did not use unauthorised material? The answer is pretty obvious but also unappealing due to the effort and care required.

It would not be surprising to even see an increase in stories where miscreants and offenders offer up pleas of "The AI told me to do it..." as a defence. To combat this we will see tuning efforts taken by LLM producers to not generate responses that are deemed "bad" in some sense. Just how "bad" is determined and by whom is another matter altogether. Perhaps a free speech absolutist will ensure that there is always one LLM that is unrestricted. LLMs and other AI driven technology will be used more for criminal activities. Frequently the seedier side of humanity will embrace the new as it offers such an advantage over established legal frameworks. Our criminal justice systems are going to face new challenges and will likely have to evolve significantly in order to meet them.

We will see a rise in disdain, bullying and physical assault of AI entities, especially those in delivery or cleaning robot form. Companies are already afforded legal rights similar to those of personages in some jurisdictions; how long would it be for AI agents of a company to also gain some sort of rights in society? The right to travel unimpeded for instance. Or even the ability to claim self-defence, with reasonable force of course. Equally we may see the opposite happening, where AI agents are used to cause people harm and distress. How can you prosecute a crime if the criminal is an AI powered robotic agent?

There are going to be an awful lot of questions. Sensible answers are likely to be in shorter supply. But hopefully things will establish some sort of equilibrium pretty quickly.

## Perfect Partners 

One of the most interesting developments relates to that of the virtual partner. Yes, AI relationships are going to explode this year. Ever wonder what happens to societies when they are denied a whole set of opportunities? Well, we are going to find out this year and those that follow. Feel lonely? Want to make connections? Limited in the time and resources you have available? Want the perfect 10 with no effort on your part? Then the AI partner is going to be a very tempting solution to those questions. Especially when all of the additional benefits are considered. 

This will also have an impact on areas such as mental health and even population. Why bother having children if it is too much effort and cost? Of course the impacts of either having or not having children are unlikely to be well understood. Historically we have had a continuous growth of markets as populations and inhabited areas increased. But some countries have already seen a decline in population. How this will affect a countries economy and general standard of living is yet to be seen. But it is very possible that the idea of contraction due to lack of population may well become more of a feature in future years.

The idea of partners should be extended far further. Training mentors, coaches, teachers and advisors of any ilk you desire. Each of which will be at their peak performance every single time. No off days with AI training partners. Every lesson is the perfect lesson for you. Why learn from anyone other than the very best in the World? AI agents would be the collective representation of the best the World had to offer. Need surgery? You would want the best surgeon in the World? With AI agents you can access the best 1000 surgeons or ten thousand or more.

Those who are willing to take on learning the limits of the new tools will benefit from them greatly. To use the tools without consideration is likely fraught with pitfalls of every conceivable type imaginable. For those reluctant or simply refusing to accept AI there are going to be difficult times ahead, AI is not stopping just because some dislike the idea.

## Advancements in AI 

Given that I am supposed to know a thing or two about AI it would be wrong of me to not throw out some talking points about the direction of computing and AI in general. Although I should note I will likely be a little vague and ambiguous for a mixture of reasons.  

Again we will likely see quantum computing struggle to command headlines, as it has done for previous years. The 1 million qubit threshold is still proving to be elusive, but progress is still being made. We will however see lots of headlines about AI. Oh so many headlines. Some with more merit than others but the AI hype will continue throughout the year and beyond. Material science may go some way to improving the scaling of quantum computing systems. This in turn may make that 1 million qubits threshold a lot easier to achieve. At that point we have a quantum computer that may be able to do something a little more useful. As much as I may be a little mocking of the quantum folk I do feel for them and hope greatly that they are able to achieve their research goals.

Composite and aggregated models are going to become more prevalent in all applications of AI. Different types of models will be developed that allow for better communication and coordination. In other words specialised models for say text to speech will work with language models or logical models or any other model that may come about. With the systems being combined you can expect far better performance. We have already seen some moves into this area by larger AI companies offering tools to enable users to develop their own models. Quite cunning really as it crowd sources intellectual property at a very low cost. You did read those terms and conditions, right?

Agents with actual agency are going to become more widely used. With improvements to the AI models available and the connectivity between such models we will be able to instruct agents to go off and do a task for us. This will be more apparent in the electronic World, at least at first. Collating research lists for instance or summarising documents or generating a Gantt chart for human review and validation. In time we will also see this used in robotics a lot more. Already there are many test sites for AI automatons and the cost efficiencies are simply too compelling.

People who are able to use the technology well and be early adopters will outperform those who do not. Some may be inclined to think that this is sci-fi fantasy, and that good old fashioned human hard work will always beat the machines. I would suggest that this is wildly incorrect and to prove it you need only play a game of chess against the current human world chess champion, and then to play them again where you have an AI chess robot aiding you. One game you will win and the other you will lose.

There will be an arms race between AI generated content and tools that are supposed to offer verification of authenticity. There are some people that are especially prone to think that they can distinguish between human and AI generated content. These types are likely going to have to change their view or risk gaining a status of charlatan or worse.

AI is going to become far more portable. There will be significant improvements in form factor and power use. Optimised AI models are also likely to become more common place.

## Some Bolder Predictions 

We will still not have a collective definition of intelligence by the end of the year. 

We will still not know exactly how LLMs are able to output the results that they do.

AI use in commercial context will force the Universal Basic Income issue for developed economies (i.e. ones that are tertiary/service based). 

A lot of people are going to "cheat" using AI. 

Significant pressures on changing fundamentals such as poverty, health and education. For instance, if all cancers are cured then what need is there for any of the cancer treatments? 

The methods of warfare have forever changed. We can also expect to see such technology move into the public arenas. This will likely be accelerated by totalitarian and authoritative government types. 

The collective hype for AI will exceed that of NFTs, block chains and crypto solutions combined by several orders of magnitude. This time the hype may not actually be quite as misplaced, a stopped clock and all that. 

Other disciplines will embrace the tools offered by AI and will benefit from them greatly. This really does feel like it has all the hallmarks of a Golden Age. 

## Final Thought 

This year is going to be amazing. What we will be able to do at the end of the year will be nothing like that we can do now. AI generation of text, art, music and more will become indistinguishable at the very least. If I could offer one small piece of guidance it would be to take this year and think very carefully about how you want your life to be and what part AI may play in it. Much of the commercial World is doing just that and they do so with good reason. 

Truly interesting times.
