# AI Strategy for SMEs for 2025

Throughout 2024 the talk of AI has been excessive. AI has been attached to everything. Has AI, is built by AI, or perhaps just a brief chance encounter on the street with AI. It all counts when it comes to presenting the products and service offerings of any company. But what the real and potential benefits are of using AI technologies is mired in intentionally vague promises and headlines. In this piece I intend to offer some sort of overview of what an AI strategy may look like for the small business.

Firstly, it is necessary to have a brief recap on what AI means as we head into 2025. In the simplest of terms, AI is everywhere. Entertainment listings, news preferences, search preferences, email systems, alerts, reminders and wellness applications on a plethora of devices. Search engines even offer AI enabled results in many instances and we see AI assistants/agents in an ever-increasing number of forms. There are so many options and so much movement that it can easily be overwhelming. The number of academic papers alone released each month on AI advancements runs into thousands.

Many commercial solutions also have AI integrated at fundamental levels. Copilot from Microsoft is a core feature of the operating system for instance. But this is also the case for just about every piece of software and peripheral you can think of. Photo/graphical editing software, Digital Audio Workbenches (DAW), collaboration software and so many more. To be fair to many of the AI solutions are valid business scenarios and vendors are rightly improving their products.

The complexity and hype around AI systems is also only seemingly increasing, if such a thing is possible. "Agentic systems" are being touted as a new form of worker for efficiency hungry organisations. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and others are all heavily invested in these services. Already we are seeing new "HR Agents" being evaluated on the likes of LinkedIn. Businesses that require customer support functions are also experimenting with agentic frameworks.

But as a business owner there is an ever-increasing demand on having to incorporate a greater number of technologies with increasing complexity and nuance. This quite simply is draining. Or of course you can simply ignore it all and pretend that the World is not changing. There comes a point quite early in the existence of a business where you start to wonder who is working for who. You can just realise that you are planning your months with time scheduled off to deal with suppliers who are supposed to be making your life easier. Of course, in many instances, at least historically, it was still quicker than doing it yourself.

## Time = Money

This is where we will start in building an AI strategy for a small business, in fact likely any business. Just how do you value your time as a business owner? This should generally be a monetary value. Now how does that compare to the national average? How many hours are you really working on building your business and how many are spent on maintaining it? In working out these numbers you need to be brutally honest. Every significant action you take associated to the business should be recorded and have a start and end time to build a true picture of where your time is going. This is not to suggest you do this forever, just simply for long enough to have a very good idea of exactly where your time goes.

Whatever the number you came to is increase it by ten. When you grow your business to the point where you are nearing that number then increase it by ten again. Do this for as long as you want or need to. The intention here is to really focus on your time as being very important and valuable. There will be lots of people throughout the course of business that will be looking to take time away from you. Respect your time.

Oddly enough AI can help greatly in tracking these types of activities and making them a lot less painful than human effort alone. There are many applications that can even be run on a phone that will assist you in tracking such tasks. These applications can also have the functionality to output a CSV (Comma Separated Values) so that you can use it within an application such as Excel. It should also be noted that applications such as Excel also have their own set of AI features that can assist in finding "insights" in your data. Another quick and easy way to see your data in a form that can be more useful. More on this later.

## Your AI Buddy

Over 2024 we have seen a massive increase in the number of AI assistants available. A host of companies all offering very similar products with increasing complexity and capability. Paid web services or embedded features of operating systems, they are everywhere. In many instances the actual performance of even the free local AI Large Language Models (LLMs) such as Mistral or Ollama is comparable to premier subscription-based services. Some would even argue that the open-source models are even superior in many instances as with the LLMs offered by Meta (owner of Facebook).

Which model is the right one for you is very dependent on you. Unfortunately, this is where you will need to invest time in practising with the technology. Just as with learning a musical instrument or a new language there is going to be an awful lot of mistakes. But you can only get better by putting in the initial work. Thankfully there are some shortcuts we can now take given that we have so many AI agents to choose from.

But before getting to selecting our own AI buddy it is important to note a few things. The most important is that the AI agents are amazingly clever and stupid at the same time. In many instances you can ask it a question or to perform a task and it will respond with the perfect answer. At other times it will return a nonsensical fabrication. The best analogy I can immediately think of is to have a friend who has a perfect memory for information. However, the information is not complete and some of it is wrong through error or by intent. Additionally, this friend will feel compelled to always answer and to have opinions and behaviours that can be hidden from you.

Now that may seem a tad dangerous and sinister to the point where you would reconsider even letting AI into your life at all. To that I would simply say, we are just using AI to work out where our time is spent and not looking for global domination. The point is to simply be mindful that AI assistants can have attributes and behaviours that must be checked to ensure accuracy.

## Finding the Perfect AI Partner

The choices available are considerable. All teasing slightly different levels of performance and capability. Just how do you go about finding the right one for you? There are also other considerations in cost, longevity, accessibility, privacy and much more.

Perhaps the best approach is to use a mixture. This is where the simplest of notepad applications comes into play. There are some very simple questions that have a great deal of utility. "What is the best way to...?" for instance. It can be applied to a vast array of scenarios, and it will always offer a starting point for building your own understanding.

If we were to expand upon this for our own immediate needs, it may form into something like:

AI Prompt - "What is the best way to find an AI chatbot I can use in a browser?"

And when asking this question, you are likely to also consider what search engine you will even use? Will it be Google, Bing or even one of the newer AI powered search bots as offered by the likes of Perplexity? You can even take this as a question to ask using search engines.

AI Prompt - "What are the best search engines to use?"

It is in this type of approach that AI offers to give almost superpower like performance. As much as this sounds like hyperbole it is not actually the case. You can ask a question and scour the entirety of human knowledge in ways that would have taken years mere decades ago. Not only that, but you can also ask for descriptions in any form you desire. That legal contract you just received can be reduced to ever simpler terms as needed. The manual for the new equipment you just received can be added and referenced using natural language.

## A Repeatable Strategy

The ability to track your time to find out exactly how it is being spent is something that continually has a value. It is an operation you are likely going to want to perform more than once in the lifetime of your company. It is also not unreasonable to think that you may also want to apply it to understanding other roles and functions within your company.

Re-evaluating what tools and services are also available to you should also be an exercise you repeat at intervals. Or even checking a marketplace to ensure that you are getting the best possible deal. To even reconsider what AI technologies you use should form part of any continued purchase review process. Are you getting the best value from material suppliers for instance? These are some of the areas where AI automation opportunities are likely to exist.

Sticking with the one task I know that I will always need and be applying to my life, where just does all my time go? We know we want to collect data about our habits and to then perform some analysis to work out where our time goes. How much data we collect and how detailed it should be is a matter of choice. But generally, more is more useful in most cases.

This is a great time to get some help with the task at hand with our new AI buddies.

AI Prompt - "What is the best way to collect data on my work habits such as travel times, project tasks, meetings and similar?"

What results you will get will depend on the LLM service or search engine you used. Other factors may also apply depending on a host of unknowns. But the thing to note is that LLMs can be very good search engines. Especially those with the ability to perform web searches and to then summarise the results.

AI Prompt - "Are there any free open-source time tracking apps that output data to CSV?"

There are plenty of options and they are also likely to change and advance over time. What is great today may not be tomorrow. Also, the solution that suites your own specific needs should be selected. One of the greatest features of humanity is its variance and that means it is rare for a singular solution to subjective needs.

At this point you may be thinking "Now I have loads of text to read across a lot of different browser tabs, where is the time to process all of that coming from?". Historically you would have to manually go through each of the results and perform your own collation and validation. Today you can just copy and paste all the answers into a single document and then ask your new AI buddies to collate and simplify all of the answers. The new agentic typed AI assistants will be able to do even more with even simpler instructions. "Check the top 10 results from the top 5 search engines for purchasing ....?" will become a common occurrence and greatly reduce research times.

## Caution is Required

With great power comes great responsibility, said some chap once-upon-a-time. It can be very tempting to start to think that the prompts you use are incredibly robust, infallible even. This is not the case and will likely never be. This relates more to just how the AI technologies work in conjunction with what humanity thinks it knows. This is a philosophical point that is best covered elsewhere, and I have written about it more than once on this site. The important bit in respect to an AI SME strategy is that you need to ensure that you perform due diligence in order to minimise risk and maximise opportunities.

Just how do you know what AI tools to trust when directing your business? This is a very subjective issue. It really does depend on any number of factors that are likely unique to you. What is your primary business language, location, region? Do the AI models return results that are appropriate for your particular needs and industry?

This is where your own personal testing plan (rubric if you will), comes into play. You create a series of questions that relate to you and your business. You can then ask as many AI tools as you like for a response. You can score each AI tool on the results and see what one(s) you prefer. Remember, you do not just need to rely on one single AI tool. You can use as many as you deem necessary. Caution is required though in order to ensure that you are not distracted from your business by this task alone. In fact, this is likely a good idea to keep in mind as it is very easy to just use AI tools to repeat a task thinking that you are actually being productive for your business. In other words, just how many cat pictures do you really need for that new pet basket promotion?

The contents of your rubric can be as varied as you like. Again, your new AI buddy can help you here. You can even ask all your AI tools open questions such as:

AI Prompt: "What is a good set of questions to use as a rubric for testing an AI tool?"

Will the results be complete, accurate, useful? Who knows! This is where your own expertise comes into play. You are still in charge and make the decisions. The AI tooling is simply a way to accelerate you to a place where it is easier to form a decision. This is especially true where there is a lot of complexity present.

If your business relies on mathematics and data analysis, then you want AI models that perform well in those fields. If you are more creative in your endeavours, then you want tooling that supports those activities. The AI tools being developed and deployed now already have "multi-modal" capabilities. In other words, they are able to handle different types on input such as natural language, documents, pictures and more. This can leave you with the impression that they are able to do more than they actually can, so caution again here.

## On With the Plan!

Now after an awful lot of preamble, as ever, I intend to deliver on the promise offered by the title of this piece.

Every business should have at least one plan. Some organisations are likely to have more than one. One for themselves, one for the bank, one for investors. Just how detailed or expansive your own business plan is will depend again on who you are and your specific needs. Plans are very useful in keeping us on track and ensuring we manage risks and seek rewards.

The following is how I am currently approaching my own AI strategy. There are likely many opportunities for great improvements on these plans as I made a point of not using any of my own AI buddies for this task. This is due to me wanting to become a better thinker and writer. The only way to do that is by effort and likely frequent failure. As much as AI tools can be a source of inspiration, they can also be a source of constraint if relied on too heavily. Frequently I write down my own thoughts as completely as I can and then use AI tools if needed. Completing thoughts can often take months and years but they are the source of new knowledge.

## Short Term Objectives

Generally, a year is not a bad time frame to use as a measure for the short-term period. This may change depending on the industry, supply chain considerations, legislation and others. But in most instances a year is fine and allows for seasonal observations to take place.

Just quite where your time goes has already been spoken of at length. But this is the basis for where your focus should be to maximise the rewards of your effort.

Once effort/time has been identified it is possible to perform some simple analytics to see where costs can be reduced and turnover and profit increase. The best candidates will be time consuming operations that are relatively simple and repeated on a daily/weekly/monthly etc basis. These are ideal for automation or even outsourcing, costs permitting and sufficient rewards.

Freshen up or check/test marketing and sales materials. This can include text, graphics and even animations. Want to create your own hold music with custom lyrics tailored for your business? A few minutes away and for free with AI tooling. This is where one of the real strengths of AI is present. Its ability to enable people with a creative idea to very quickly and easily create without the laborious and often years of effort required to become proficient in art, music, literature and more. Is it great and amazing and will win artistic awards? Probably not. But is it good enough? A surprisingly low bar in many cases.

Collate company documentation and identify and correct any gaps, inconsistencies and errors. Ensure that customer support scripts and FAQs are of a suitable quality and sufficiently detailed.

Market knowledge is easier to collate and summarise. No longer is there a need to visit industry news site after site. You can have an AI agent summarise the publications you wish to track. It can even advise you of new publications that may come into play. This can also extend to other aspects of market intelligence. Customer behaviours, supply chain efficiencies, competitor analysis and much more besides.

These are but a few possible options and just exactly what works for you is a matter of choice. But whereas historically all this effort would likely fall to just you this is no longer the case. You now have your AI buddy. They are ideally suited to help you navigate your own set of wants and needs. So rather than follow the examples here why not use the approach of letting AI systems brainstorm with you? Perhaps a good initial question is:

AI Prompt - "What are the most important aspects of running a business in...?"

Quite what you get as a result will depend on what AI system you use. Of course, you can change the example to any business you like and even to add context and constraints such as geographical regions or target market audiences. The main point of this exercise is to act as a quick sense check that you have considered the important elements of your business. You may not agree with the lists provided by the AI or have additions you think are crucial, and this is fine and should absolutely be the case. Some of the very best results we see so far in the use of AI is when humans and AI systems work in conjunction with each other. If you doubt that at all I am very willing to play anyone of your choice at chess and to utterly crush them. Do note I do not actually play chess, but I do know an AI that does.

Good questions have a value. Make sure that the questions you ask are kept and secured from error or loss. This can massively reduce the effort required should a task need to be repeated. Just drop all your questions into your AI buddy and ask them to answer each in turn. Cherry-pick the bits that apply best to you as you see fit. A task that would often take weeks can now be reduced to a few hours. The excuses not to do it is greatly diminished.

Short term plans are often where most of the immediate effort of a business is focused. It is also where the low-hanging-fruit of AI powered efficiencies often resides. If you know where your time is going and what tasks are simple and repeatable you have the perfect setup to ask good questions of an AI assistant. If you can save a few hours a week even it will scale throughout the year and beyond. That few hours soon turn into another month of productivity.

## Medium Term Objectives

This frequently will look to a 1-to-3-year period from the current position. A perhaps useful way to think about this time scale is to consider the ability to grow your business. Do you have everything in order so that you can scale operations comfortably? Do you have financing in place for any capital expenditure? Will new markets be present or existing ones diminishing? What is your capability in changing direction should circumstances dictate? Of course you may be perfectly happy with the business, income streams and stability that already exists and have no desire to change it, and in that you would be most correct. Your business should ultimately work for you and not the other way around.

But it is also important to note the transformations that can take place and the speed at which they can happen. The advancements during 2024 in the AI space has been tremendous. AI influences have resulted in two Nobel awards, one for physics and one for chemistry. Large Language Models (LLMs such as ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot/Claude etc) are being extended so that they can better answer increasingly complex questions. Once these models are improved you can expect them to outperform any human in an increasing number of domains.

With the potential for marketplaces to change from technological advancements it may be useful to stay very focused on satisfying customer needs and to add value such as customer experience, personalisation/customisation and more. LinkedIn are already offering AI HR Agents and customer support AIs are becoming more common place.

AI systems are also very useful when it comes to monitoring market intelligence. Legislation is proposed before being enacted and there are opportunities to prepare for and even influence it. This can also apply to fields of research such as medicine or material composition.

## Long Term Objectives

This is where the company wants to be in the future. Do you want an international household brand name products and supply chain, or would you prefer an unknown company aside from the market you supply goods/services to? Do you want to diversify? Secure lines of supply and have vertical and horizontal business strategies? Even to sell the business as an ongoing concern may be under consideration. Again, the choice is a personal one to make and one that is likely to change or morph at least slightly over time.

But big plans and ideas frequently require time to complete. Concrete takes time to pour and set, even with advancements in 3D structure printing there are still the fundamental laws of physics that must be obeyed. Planning, land acquisition and a host of other obligations may also present themselves depending on the type of business.

Up until a year or so ago I would have been inclined to just leave the rest of this piece as is. But given everything that has happened in 2024 I would ask that you take but even a moment to consider the impossible. Just exactly what would you do if you were to wake up and have access to anything you want/need at effectively zero cost? What would your life be like and what would you want to do in the World? There are many voices stating that new forms of superior AI will become available in the next five years. If we save so much of our time with the use of technology, then just exactly what do we do?

## Your Race to Run

Whether or not you decide to use AI or not is your choice to make. It may seem tempting to even just defer thinking about AI too much and to wait and see what the market decides. Historically this has not been a bad choice as even "rapid change" would be measured in years and decades. I would argue that things appear to be moving more at pace than at any other time in history. Probably relates to having more scientists alive doing science things than at any point in history before.

Given the types of advancements we are likely to see and the massive advantages they are likely to give over those who do not embrace such technologies it seems foolish to ignore AI altogether.

Often, I have found that people are reluctant to try new things for not wishing to be judged or to look foolish. The very best bit about LLMs and many of the associated technologies is that they do not care. You can ask stupid questions and when you still do not understand you can keep asking the LLM to explain it in ever simpler terms. It will not give up on you! Or more importantly, mock you in the staff break room later.

It is hoped that I have been able to show just how AI can be applied in this article. More importantly I hope I have been able to inspire you to try this out for yourselves. Humans working with AI are a very powerful combination that will give far better results almost every single time.
