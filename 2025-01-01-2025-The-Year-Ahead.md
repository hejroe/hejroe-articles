# 2025 – The Year Ahead

2025 is going to be a year of truth. A somewhat obtuse turn of phrase that likely needs a bit of an explanation. So let's delve... err I mean let me try to describe exactly what that is. Truth is a very tricky little beast. In the simplest of terms it relates to facts and this other thing called reality.

But once you try to work out what those words mean you are opening a Pandora's Box of other complexities. Just what is a fact and just what is reality? Both pretty big questions that will not easily be resolved. But they can be tamed somewhat by making some really quite sweeping generalisations and assumptions. Ahh yes, assumptions! What could possibly go wrong?

Perhaps one of the quickest ways I can convey my meaning is to pick on one of my favourite topics. Round Earth versus Flat Earth "debate". There are a number of experiments that can be conducted by anyone at any time from pretty much anywhere that can show the Earth to be round. Technologies have been developed on the scientific principles used and are performed thousands and thousands of times a day/hour/minute. Technologies such as communications (satellites), exploration, navigation and a host of others all "proving" that the Earth is round.

Whereas the number of experiments that prove the Earth is flat is zero.

The idea that there is some sort of debate or argument to be had is somewhat fallacious, deceitful even. So why do so many seem to think that a flat Earth could exist? The fact that some will consider it funny to be able to convince others that Earth is flat is a factor that should be noted. Also another would be those who derive pleasure from misleading and controlling others. Some even sell "merch".

In this brief example I hope to show slither of humanity. The things that make us human and have got us this far are not always rational or clear. They should not be dismissed and discarded without a second thought, but we need to be mindful that they can be a weakness to our collective intellect and effort. The Contrarian exists for a reason of some kind and may functionally offer more of a positive than a negative. Having a mechanism to always keep some instability in an otherwise calm system can be a great way to expose edge cases and observe black swan events. As I have written before, I think that with the UK being so changeable in climate gives rise to more thinking and planning. Generally, the more of a thing you do the more proficient you become at it. A lot of invention has come from the UK in one form or another. This is likely wistful thinking on my part, and I am far too busy to investigate it further at this time. But I digress.

## A Common Truth

Finding common ground can be achieved. There are things that we can do that can even help us build up a picture of the World that we can have an agreed sense of proof. Take for instance the notion of gravity. You have a thing, and you drop it from a height and as long as there are no impeding forces gravity will make it fall to the ground. You can do it, I can do it, anyone can do it and all with the same results. Now just quite how gravity works or what it exactly is can be something that people can and will disagree upon. But the actual observed evidence is repeatable.

There are other simple experiments that anyone can do in order to prove some fundamental truth of our perceived existence. All the results basically come to the same conclusion every time with precision normally being the only difference. Materials doing certain things under specific temperatures and pressures such as water freezing or boiling. Copper, table salt and calcium all burn different colours against a flame test. This is something that we can do, observe and record and come to a similar agreement. Even when exceptions in sensory perception such as colour-blindness comes into play.

It could also be said that there are many moral imperatives that are true. People need water, air, food, shelter and a host of other things. How we deal with such things as societies and even as a species is a reflection on us. What we do in the World defines us, not what we say we will do or internalised intent.

However, we should also be mindful of the complete range of humanity. As much as someone can see a stranger and think that they are the most perfect thing to have even existed there is also the exact opposite in existence. Ever meet someone who you just thought was terrible or worse? That is the inverse of the one you liked. Again, I must stress that this is a range and different for different people. In other words, sometimes our reasoning is irrational and based on primitives we are not even conscious of.

## Cheap Watch Versus Atomic Clocks

A cheap watch is exactly that. Will it keep time accurately to the second for ten thousand years? Probably not. But does it need to? Could even the fact it manages to roughly be right around midday good enough? Atomic clocks are another matter altogether. Generally less wearable for starters. Precision comes at a price and so does speed. This is going to be a lesson that features in the AI ecosystem throughout 2025.

Not everything requires a super-duper-all-knowing-all-singing-and-dancing LLM. As I write this I cannot help think of poor Marvin from Hitch Hikers Guide to the Galaxy; "Here I am, brain the size of a planet, and they ask me to take you to the bridge. Call that job satisfaction, 'cause I don’t.". There are many times where I just need a simple quick thing to happen such as taking a note/reminder or other trivial task. Using massive computational resources over a cloud network is just wasteful for something that can be resolved so easily locally. The power of a well-crafted "IF" statement should never be underestimated.

In other words, I think that the year will see a bit of sanity return to the hardware scene. Already there are signs of cooling and once all the big players have finished training their super-sized models they will want to offload all that GPU compute that they have. Especially if they tapped into nuclear or greener energy alternatives. Already we have seen a rise in SLMs (Small Language Models) as being the glue between different levels of task complexity. Equally advancements in general techniques see smaller models being only slightly worse in some instances when compared to far larger and more reputable models. Just how smart does my POS (Point of Sale) terminal need to be?

## Trust Me Bro'

Some will be actively hoping that you rely on their truth. Imagine a scenario where you have a programmer looking to resolve a particular problem. They instruct their chosen AI powered LLM coding assistant to write the program as specified. The code is generated, tested and then incorporated into the business.

"IF building_alarm_armed THEN light_off ELSE light_on" is the code that would have been required to resolve the problem. But for some reason the AI powered LLM coding assistant seems to have generated code that has deployed monitoring agents across the whole network and incorporated complex reporting API components into "the cloud provider". A simply vast amount of resources are being consumed due to trusting the solution offered. It is almost as if the AI coding assistant actually gave a response that solved the problem but was beneficial to the owner of the LLM model. How could such a fantastical scenario ever occur?

In addition to that is the potential for other forms of influence and interference. Care will be needed of what question you ask in specific countries and similar care is required for answers. This is where the biggest change is likely to happen. People are going to have an option to shift from a landscape where facts and opinions are formed for them from talk shops parading as news or informed discussion. You can create your own show locally with your own host(s) and guest(s) as you see fit talking of things from sources that you consider worthy of input and fact checked real time, if you should so desire.

The AI systems being developed at this time allow for a greater level of scrutiny than was previously thought possible. Finding things out and then proving them is quite simply expensive. If you want to see what is on top of a mountain then you have to get up there somehow. Technology changes this cost profile. Landing a helicopter on top of a mountain is easier in many respects than walking up it.

Now this piece is supposed to be about 2025 and you may be wondering why I have just been talking about truth instead of the normal topics being covered by other pundits? This is simply because the tools to allow for people to develop their own sense of truth based on their own personal perspectives and requirements are going to become more prevalent. More importantly such tools are going to perform incredibly well. Just imagine real time conversations being analysed where statements are checked for truth. That sales pitch where they make certain claims on performance and reliability can be checked real time. Complex contracts can be scrutinised section by section and for a summary to be provided that will be far more useful than the often one-sided affairs. The possibilities here are simply giddying, to me at least.

## Likely Events

Not wanting to disappoint those who may be reading this and were thirsting after more 2025 AI industry insights I will offer these as some recompense.

* AI LLM models are going to continue to be refined and reduced in size.
* Smaller models will be run on smaller form factors such as phones, notebooks, laptops, other wearables, robots/drones and more.
* Specialised highly tuned models will become more common. Maintenance, training coaches, self-help and talking therapies.
* "Level 2 thinking" systems will become expensive outsourced systems likely specialised and available on a compute/hour basis. These will likely be blended into a "seamless user experience" as we have already seen from Apple Inc in 2024.
* Legislation will still be unresolved in all of its forms. Copyright/IP issues will exist, and regulation will dither/collapse.
* Probability of a black swan event is very high. With how science is advancing in many areas and with more scientists now than at any point in history there is a real chance that someone/something is going to discover something monumental. Now quite what that will be is another matter. But with AI systems effectively winning two Nobel Prizes in 2024 you should be hard pressed to argue that AI systems have no value. I am still very hopeful that a solution to the quantum computing problem will be found. With the advancements in material science, it would not be unreasonable to think that there could be a stable material that had room temperature like properties. Biology has proof that such quantum systems can exist. The taming of graphene would also be very cool. The best aspect of this is that such an advancement could come from a single researcher or smallest of businesses.
* Access to tooling is going to improve. Despite many organisations trying to encourage some sort of lock-in with customers, there is going to be an improving ecosystem of open tools that allow businesses to host and write their own AI solutions. It is very possible that this can even be done completely with natural language. We are still not quite there yet but some services have highly specialised and functional AI systems that do exactly what is needed.
* Better data is going to become more valuable, especially those systems able to evaluate "better data".
* People and organisations that adapt AI technologies in a well thought out manner are going to perform very well.

## Interesting Times Ahead

Perhaps the biggest prediction I can make is actually very much a non-prediction. At this time I simply do not quite know how things will pan out for the next year. Previous years felt easier to offer actionable insights. Spring is followed by Summer and so on. Easy to do. But now, far less so. At least in the sense of general technological advancements.

I am thinking of 2025 as a “Year of Truth” in many respects. This “truth” is going to change for many, or at least another option will present itself. “Truth” is going to become a thing that is derived from a person’s perspective and not just delivered from external sources. In other words, our perceived idea of the World is going to become less reliant on ideas/opinions that are made by others such as news outlets or influencers. Those that embrace this idea are likely to be able to move with greater confidence than those who do not.

What I can say though is that there has never been a better time to be a learner, doer, thinker and all the other things that makes us humans human. I had an amazing 2024 and I am incredibly excited for what I plan to do this coming year with my company. But news of that will have to wait for the Foresight Analytics yearly review that I intend to publish next. The closing point here is that there are now a new set of tools that can help every creator, explorer, entertainer and more. Do stuff.
