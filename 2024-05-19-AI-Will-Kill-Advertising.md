# AI Will Kill Advertising
 
At least in the sense that we understand it today. This is a conclusion I have come to after thinking about how current advancements in technology are continuing to accelerate. Again this feels like one of those bold statements that fall apart once examined in even a little more detail. So let's examine my thinking and see where I can be wrong.

## Conventional Advertising

Advertising is something that comes in many forms. All manner of different shapes, sizes and intents. Each vying for your attention with the aim of influencing your behaviour so that you make purchasing decisions that are beneficial to the vendor, and sometimes even to you. At times this may be advertising that you ask for as you have a need that a purchase will satisfy. But perhaps more frequently is the advertising that is not sought after. These forms of advertising that demand attention are diverse and at times even manipulative.

Each form of advertising relies on certain types of contact and how advertising messages are conveyed will vary depending on the media type being used. A billboard approach is only able to show its message to an audience that passes it by. Adverts returned in a set of internet search results are only done so by the search agent being used, say Google, Bing or Yahoo. Newspapers, radio, podcasts, streaming platforms and more all have different methods of advertising that can be applied.

A great deal of effort is put into advertising and marketing as a whole. Being able to present your products and services to a marketplace is crucial to almost all businesses. But being able to influence your prospective customers is even more valuable when it comes to a return on investment.

The value of advertising is reflected in the annual spend and also the profits of the largest advertising organisations. Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft and many more are all in the advertising business and advertising revenue forms a significant income stream.

There are tools that have existed for years that help us avoid advertising. Advert blockers exist in a number of different guises. Even routers can be configured to be a blackhole where unwanted adverts are directed to. Email spam filters have become increasingly effective. In general people may even be slightly better at being able to ignore advertisements than in the past. After all repeated exposure often leads to desensitisation of some sort.

## The Future Will Not Be Built For Advertising

I will be able to avoid advertising in the future. If this seems like fantasy just consider for a moment an AI agent that reads/watches/reviews any information you choose to consume. This agent is held on a local computing device, so no cloud service to subscribe to. Its sole purpose is to remove any adverts from the media intended to be consumed. That podcast you have listened to for years with embedded adverts can have those stripped out by the agent.

As soon as you choose content via a website address the content is preloaded and examined. Anything deemed unwanted can simply be removed or altered. That product placement in a film for a particular drink can just be replaced with a plainer beverage. Or it could even be altered to a competitor’s product. Augmented Reality devices will be able to alter what you see real time, just as they already do. But the level of sophistication will be greatly improved. Unsightly objects and intrusions of any kind can be filtered out. Have every passerby look like your favourite music idol or movie star. The wisdom of some of this can easily be questioned, but technical limitations will not. Much of this is already being done, albeit in a more primitive form. Perhaps the future will even consist of completely generated content. This is not a new thought by any means, but the point does hold true. What if you can just generate the content you want to hear, see or experience? If you want a fusion of films and styles, then you can generate one by command. If you approve of it, then you can even pass it onto others free of any constraints in intellectual property. All with no advertising required. Music, films, amusing sketches could all be dynamically generated locally. If you wanted to share something you enjoyed, you can simply exchange the necessary prompts or metadata with those you want.

## Is The War Over?

Does this mean that the money being spent by advertising institutions is being wasted? This is a perfectly reasonable question to ask. And this is something I do not quite know the answer to. The reason for this is that being able to influence someone is incredibly powerful thing to do. To make someone decide something that is advantageous to you is a bold action in the World. One that should perhaps come with more consequence than it does.

But with the advent and evolution of localised AI agents acting on the behalf of its human agent we may possibly negate all forms of advertising. The AI agent will help "protect" their human counterpart from their own humanity and inclination to be persuaded by less than sincere means.

Perhaps those who manufacture goods and services will have to find new ways to overcome the additional barriers to their intended target audience. Would it be unreasonable for the AI agent to even seek out goods and services based on their own understanding of their human agents needs? If the human agent needs new socks, then it is in the best interests of the AI agent to seek out the "best socks"?

## Or Has The War Just Begun?

An arms race of sorts has been conducted for years already. Historically it could be said that advertising was evaded by going to make a cup of tea while the adverts were on during your favourite shows commercial break. As advertising techniques and forms of media advanced so did the technology to try and filter unsolicited adverts. This is especially the case with ad blockers that are used to filter out unwanted adverts from web activities.

Then there is the "valued customer" approach being used. Streaming services or memberships can be purchased to circumvent advertising. Although it seems that the temptation of also gaining money from advertisements creates a creep that ultimately means you end up watching adverts.

There are many that talk of ways of how to ensure that the "user"/"customer" actually pays attention to the carefully crafted adverts. Eye tracking software and other means to ensure user engagement is achieved. How this develops will be interesting to see. But to have a World where your car will not start as you have not paid enough attention to the car manufacturers social platform is becoming less surprising.

We have seen adverts in search results for many years now. Something that was not as prominent or even present at all in the early days of the Internet. But over time the advantages to suppliers and search engine organisations was all too apparent. If you search for running shoe reviews, then you would see adverts for such items from the search results as well as within any review content you may choose to read. This is something that suppliers have already integrated into their various Chatbot AIs. How wise or sustainable this will be in the future is yet to be determined. But I suspect that Chatbots that do continually try to sell me something are not going to feature in my life for very long.

## Goal Alignment

One thing that we see with current advertising models is that the goals of the participants are different. This does not mean that they cannot be aligned in some way but that is not especially by design. That is to say, if an advertising entity can influence you legally by some means without it actually benefitting you then there is a misalignment in goals. Your loss is their gain. But if an organisation has any positive outcome for all parties concerned the benefits are hard to ignore.

As a consumer of goods and services I am very much part of the target of advertising organisations. But I know only too well that I will invest my time and effort into limiting the amount of my attention that is consumed by advertisers. When I need to make a purchase, I will actively seek it out. But I should also note that my patience is quite thin when encountering any behaviour that even hints as being nefarious. Generally if people want my attention, then they should pay for it. My time is precious to me and I value it greatly. There is lots I want to do in the World and dealing with advertisements in any way is not something I generally consider to be a good use of my time.
