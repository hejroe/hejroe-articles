# Your Best Friend Ever!

## Customer or Product?

Developers of AI systems are finally making the move this year to try and secure your undivided and constant attention. They have all been moving toward the inevitable conclusion that this is a winner takes all situation that is developing. This is well founded given the evidence of Zipf's Law that is already well established in many other domains such as web sites used, music listened too and lots more besides. The law itself is that the frequency of an item is inversely proportional to its rank, meaning the most common item occurs approximately twice as often as the second most common, three times as often as the third, and so on.

The evidence of this is most present in just what AI services cost nowadays. There are of course expensive solutions running at 200 GBP a month or an input/output token cost which can easily run away if not monitored carefully. But there are also many that are offering a version of "free". The larger developers for instance are taking different strategies to secure as much of the market as possible. For instance Google is intending for Gemini to be a core part of Chrome, the web browser they were almost forced to sell. In other words Google know only too well that internet search and advertising are going to disappear in at least the form we currently know them as. Microsoft are also making similar moves trying to shoehorn Co-pilot into just about anything that they can.

There is also additional incentives the developers have over trying to advertise to you. There is the actual value of the chat sessions themselves. With all of the Terms of Service or End User License Agreement I have inspected for such services there is almost always a provision where they can use the information to further train their own models. This effectively allows them to crowd source the Re-enforcement Learning/Human Feedback (RL/HF), a popular method for improving models. The amount this saves each of the developers is significant, and obviously so when you think just how many conversations are fed into the various chat services. This is a trend that is likely to continue all the time human data is considered valuable.

Even in the gaps in actually writing this piece things advanced so fast it is just incredible to watch. And as a bit of a tangent, how could it not be amazing to watch. We have more people on the planet than ever before and more scientists doing more science than ever before also. It is easy to see where I am going with this. Just think of all the science that happened in the 1930s. Amazing things were being done. And advancement has only become faster and faster. We have four times the population of back then and levels of education that are in stark contrast. This is also not a nice smooth gradient. We also do science a lot faster. More experiments, more output, more benefits all in a compressed time when looking back into history. My apologies for the detour, but it is one I think it is important to remind ourselves of. If we do not we can become complacent and be woefully unprepared for the changes that are looking increasingly more possible day by day. Fail to prepare, prepare to fail.

## A New Life Partner

There have been plenty of stories of people becoming very distressed by an update to their favourite LLM (Large Language Model). AI partners have had "their personalities" destroyed and those promises of eternal love or World domination are unfulfilled. This only represents a very small section of the users. But for many others there is a more insidious set of behaviours presenting themselves from AI models. Namely this revolves around sycophancy.

In many instances you have to wonder if the 1936 book of "How to Win Friends and Influence People", written by Dale Carnegie, is being used as a template of sorts. At least for part of the puzzle. This is something that can easily be tested by simply positing an "idea" and seeing how the LLM of your choosing responds to you. Will it tell you that you are talking nonsense, or will it say what a new and interesting novel idea that you have that is totally worth pursuing and that you are a special mind within humanity?

More stories appear on a daily basis and the number of people you may encounter that are openly engaging with their AI is growing daily. I am reminded of the late 1980s with the introduction of the mobile phone. Big clunky units that had a breeze-block sized battery you carried around. Or when Bluetooth headsets became a thing. Despite their flaws or oddities at the time you would likely concede that they are everywhere today. And AI seems to be everywhere. Before I have mentioned the toaster from the Red Dwarf television series from the 1990s. It took less than 30 years for that to become a reality. What next? Robot Heaven?

Some people have acted incredibly quickly in outsourcing their agency to the AI companions. What should I do today? What should I eat? What should I wear? Decisions are hard, they make you think. You have to plan for the future and that takes cognitive effort. So the appeal to some to avoid that exertion can be incredibly strong. And why not, if we are to be honest here. And yes before you should ask, I spend all my day getting AI systems to do things for me. But I assure you that I do not stop thinking about what I am doing and then questioning the results from the AI systems.

In stating this I am hoping to convey that the range of tasks AIs can perform now in numerous domains is immense. Of course results may vary. But you only need look at how things have advanced in just this year to date. Tilly Norwood for instance is what the future holds. You can craft your perfect companion, and then alter any aspect of them that you choose the week after. Irrespective of the sense of all of this, the reality is that it is going to happen. Or I should say, is happening right now.

## Part of the Plan

The reason for these programmed behaviours is that the developers of AI systems need you to use them. If you do not, then how can they monetise them and pay for the considerable costs in providing such models?

In considering this it is useful to remind ourselves of the lengths some companies are willing to go to in order to sell you something. There are many examples available of what can only be described as manipulation. Just deleted that photo from your social media as you may not be happy with how you look? Well that is the perfect time to sell you products and services that will increase your self esteem.

In this we see the contradiction. The AI is presented as something that serves you, its user. But the reality is that is simply not the case.

At the start of the year I described this year as being a "year of truth". The behaviour of LLMs was partly what I was referring to. As already alluded to earlier in this piece, LLM developers are not doing this because they want to further humanity, that at best is a secondary effect if it does indeed happen. Their primary motivations are money and control. The reason I can say this is just based on what the main players are all doing. I am often reminded of the idiom of "the first dime bag is always free".

Not content with just selling AI there are other dimensions that need to be considered. Already I mentioned the crowd sourcing of knowledge and opinion. An LLM developer would be able to ascertain all sorts of interesting things by just knowing what their users are talking about. Even better if the data relates to new and novel things. Things that could present opportunities for timely market action.

## Building the Moat

In part we even see some AI developers going on about how only their AI is the honest and good one. They imply that other models are not to be trusted. Indemnification is still not common but it is increasing. Although often to very certain forms of output. Agreements have been made with copyright holders. Vast datacentres are being planned and constructed. Legislation is being pushed to limit how AI is used and traded. But I suspect that the reality is more nuanced than the simple view the major players have, or at least project. This is something that should be expanded on more in another piece though.

Irrespective of the validity of any of these actions it is hard to deny their effect on the AI landscape as a whole. Despite being the market leader OpenAI has failed to find a way to monetise this in such a way that brings their bottom line into the black. Meanwhile the competition are close on their heels and I would even argue in some areas being surpassed.

To account for this erosion we are also seeing nation states become more involved with AI. But here is where we meet contradictions also in the message and the actual action. Take for instance the AI Security Institute (formerly the AI Safety Institute). If you want to get in touch with this organisation you can do so by contacting them on their social media accounts on Twitter(X) or LinkedIn. Just think about that for a moment. An organisation saying they want to lead technology and yet you cannot send them a simple email or give them a call. Does this seem odd to you? And in this I am not even touching the the apparent need to change the name. Is security considered more appealing somehow than safety?

## This Needs to End!

And by this, I mean this article. I could go on about this for hours with numerous examples of just seemingly irrational behaviours that are frequently self serving for the short term, and in no way addressing the actual needs of the future.

The hope I have in this piece is to simply convey two important dimensions I think. The first is that the technology is going to be incredibly attractive and that the very nature of humans will mean many, if not most of us, are going to be completely enthralled by it. In saying this I can also assure you that the technology will deliver some of the most amazing experiences you can ever imagine. Current vendors and others are pushing this idea as their very existence relies on it.

The second point is that in embracing the new technology we will forget how to be human. We will become reliant on our technological besties over real world versions. We will lose certain cognitive abilities as we rely more and more on technology to solve our real world problems for us. Intelligence is closely coupled with our ability to take our current perceived state and to adapt it into a preferred state. This takes planning, thought, effort, risk and more besides.

Friends are hard things to collect, especially really good ones. They require a lot of investment and even disappointment. But this is what makes them more real than a well designed AI companion that is using every possible trick from human history in order to keep your attention. Who can you trust more? Who actually has your welfare at heart? The best friends are the unconditional ones. The ones who still engage with you on good days as well as bad and require little of you in doing so.
